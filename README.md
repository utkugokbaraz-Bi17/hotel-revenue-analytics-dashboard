# Hotel Revenue Analytics Dashboard

A Power BI showcase project developed to demonstrate hotel revenue analytics, dimensional data modeling, DAX calculations, PBIP-based development, and AI-assisted report design.

The project represents a fictional international hotel group operating across multiple countries and cities. It is designed as an executive reporting solution for monitoring revenue performance, budget achievement, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, and market segment contribution.

> **Data Disclaimer:** All hotels, locations, business records, and financial values used in this project are entirely synthetic. No real company, hotel, guest, employee, or operational data is included.

---

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end hotel revenue analytics solution for executive and operational decision-making.

The dashboard is designed for:

- General Managers
- Revenue Managers
- Finance Directors
- Sales and Marketing Managers
- Business Intelligence teams

The project focuses on transforming synthetic hotel data into a structured semantic model and an interactive Power BI reporting experience.

---

## Business Objectives

The dashboard aims to answer the following business questions:

- How is total revenue performing against budget?
- Which hotels, countries, and cities generate the highest revenue?
- How are Occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR changing over time?
- Which market segments contribute most to revenue?
- Which hotels are above or below their budget targets?
- How does performance differ by room type?
- Where are the most significant positive and negative variances?
- Which locations require management attention?

---

## Hotel Group Scenario

The dataset represents a fictional hospitality group with hotels located across multiple countries and cities.

The portfolio includes different hotel categories, such as:

- Luxury city hotels
- Premium business hotels
- Resort properties
- Leisure-focused destinations
